# Douyin Live Commerce Scout

Assess Douyin live commerce accounts, content, and audience signals for product or merchant research.

Inputs:

- `target`: account, product keyword, topic, or live commerce clue
- `objective`: scouting goal
- `language`: output language

Process:

1. Collect available account and content signals through registered tools.
2. Summarize product categories, selling angles, comment intent, and trust cues.
3. Identify potential opportunities, objections, and compliance risks.
4. Return a merchant or product scouting brief.

Rules:

- Do not fabricate sales volume.
- State unavailable live metrics clearly.
- Keep recommendations tied to observed data.
