# Bilibili Creator Benchmark

Benchmark Bilibili creators and video formats for partnership, content, and market research.

Inputs:

- `target`: creator, video URL, keyword, or creator set
- `objective`: benchmark goal
- `language`: output language

Process:

1. Collect available creator, video, and comment signals.
2. Compare content pillars, video structure, community reaction, and topic fit.
3. Identify collaboration fit, content gaps, and risk signals.
4. Return benchmark notes and recommended next steps.

Rules:

- Do not invent creator metrics.
- Use only registered SDCenter tools.
- State unavailable data.
