# Instagram Hashtag Research

Research Instagram hashtags and keyword clusters for market, audience, and creator discovery.

Inputs:

- `target`: hashtag, keyword, topic, or niche
- `objective`: research goal
- `language`: output language

Process:

1. Search registered Instagram tools for visible content signals.
2. Group content by niche, visual pattern, audience reaction, and creator type.
3. Identify promising angles, partnership leads, and risk themes.
4. Return a hashtag research brief.

Rules:

- Do not infer private demographics.
- Do not call unregistered external URLs.
- Mark missing evidence clearly.
