# WeChat Account Research

Research WeChat public accounts or Channels accounts for market and content workflows.

Inputs:

- `target`: account, channel, article, video URL, keyword, or topic
- `objective`: research goal
- `language`: output language

Process:

1. Resolve available public account and content signals.
2. Summarize positioning, content themes, audience reaction, and risk cues.
3. Identify partnership, content, or customer research opportunities.
4. Return an account research brief.

Rules:

- Do not fabricate private WeChat data.
- Do not call unregistered external resources.
- Separate facts from recommendations.
