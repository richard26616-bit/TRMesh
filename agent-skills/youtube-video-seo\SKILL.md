# YouTube Video SEO

Analyze YouTube videos, channels, search topics, titles, and audience comments for SEO and content strategy.

Inputs:

- `target`: channel, video URL, keyword, or topic
- `objective`: SEO or content goal
- `language`: output language

Process:

1. Collect available channel, video, and keyword signals.
2. Review title, description, topic fit, comment themes, and content structure.
3. Identify search opportunities and packaging improvements.
4. Output revised title angles, tags, risks, and next actions.

Rules:

- Do not invent search volume.
- Explain when ranking evidence is unavailable.
- Keep advice grounded in fetched data.
