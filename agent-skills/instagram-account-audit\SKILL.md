# Instagram Account Audit

Audit Instagram accounts, posts, reels, and audience signals for brand, creator, and competitor research.

Inputs:

- `target`: handle, post URL, reel URL, hashtag, or keyword
- `objective`: audit goal
- `language`: output language

Process:

1. Fetch available profile and content signals.
2. Review posting themes, creative format, engagement patterns, and comment sentiment.
3. Highlight positioning, strengths, weaknesses, and risks.
4. Recommend practical account or campaign improvements.

Rules:

- Do not fabricate private metrics.
- Cite unavailable fields as unavailable.
- Keep recommendations tied to observed evidence.
